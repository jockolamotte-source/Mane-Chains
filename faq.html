FAQ

Simple answers for choosing, wearing, and caring for your Mane Chain pieces.

How do bead installations work?

A small bead is threaded onto a section of hair and used to secure the jewelry in place.

How do clip-in pieces work?

Clip-in pieces attach in seconds and are easy to reposition or remove.

What hairstyles can I wear them with?

Mane Chain pieces can be styled with loose waves, braids, ponytails, buns, half-up styles, and formal updos.

How should I care for my hair jewelry?

Remove pieces before showering, swimming, sleeping, or applying heavy hair products directly to the jewelry. Store pieces dry and separated to help prevent tangling.
