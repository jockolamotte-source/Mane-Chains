About Mane Chain

A little playful. A little glam.

Hi, I’m Alli.

I wanted hair accessories to feel fun again.

Welcome to Mane Chain! I’m Alli, the creator, designer, and owner of this unique hair jewelry brand. As a licensed cosmetologist, I’ve always loved finding fun, creative ways to make hair feel a little more special.

I wanted to create a side hustle that combined my love for hair with something unique, and that’s how Mane Chain came to life.

I was inspired by the feathers and tinsel we loved adding to our hair when we were younger, but I wanted to give that idea a more grown-up feel. Mane Chain is my take on hair accessories—a little playful, a little glam, and still classy and effortless enough to wear anywhere.

Thanks for being here and supporting my little dream! ✨
